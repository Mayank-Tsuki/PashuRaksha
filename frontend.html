Tommy's Inspiring Journey: From Collapse to Care

Tommy was found collapsed on a busy street corner, critically ill with severe dehydration, high fever, and advanced skin mange. He was too weak to stand when caring citizens noticed him and submitted an emergency rescue alert. Within 20 minutes, a mobile vet team arrived, administered IV fluids, and transferred him to intensive care. After 6 weeks of dedicated medical treatment and nourishing care, Tommy made a complete recovery. Today, he thrives happily with his adoptive family!
